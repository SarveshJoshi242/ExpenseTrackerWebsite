Privacy Policy for Aftermath

Welcome to Aftermath ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to providing a secure, transparent, and privacy-respecting financial operating experience. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and safeguard your personal information across the Aftermath mobile app, web platform, and connected automated messaging integrations.

Core Privacy Pledge: We will NEVER sell, lease, rent, or monetize your personal transactional records to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers. Your financial logs belong solely to you.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to deliver accurate expense tracking, smart budget forecasting, and account security:

  • Account Credentials: Your display name, email address, cryptographically salted and hashed passwords, and optional profile avatar.
  • Contact & Verification Identifier: Your mobile phone number for secure One-Time Password (OTP) verification and conversational expense logging.
  • Financial Records: Transaction amounts, category classifications (e.g., Food, Travel, Utilities), payment methods (Cash, UPI, Card), ledger balances (Bharat Khata), and budget limits that you record.
  • Device Telemetry: High-level platform type and application version to ensure software compatibility and deliver secure updates.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for core application functionality:

  • To compute monthly expenditure analytics, category distributions, and envelope spending thresholds.
  • To synchronize your personal financial logs across your authenticated devices.
  • To transmit real-time verification OTP codes and scheduled 9:00 PM evening check-ins via authenticated messaging gateways.
  • To protect against account takeover, automated bot abuse, and unauthorized access.

3. Cloud Infrastructure & Encryption Standards

Aftermath utilizes high-grade cybersecurity protocols designed to safeguard your records against unauthorized access:

  • Zero-Trust Transit Encryption: All data transmissions between client applications and cloud servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Encrypted Cloud Vault Storage: User records are stored in hardened cloud facilities with strict tenant database isolation and encrypted backups.
  • Credential Protection: Passwords are never stored or transmitted in plain text and are secured using industry-standard salted hashing algorithms.

4. Authorized Third-Party Integrations

To enable specialized capabilities, Aftermath communicates with authorized platforms under strict privacy controls:

  • Meta WhatsApp Cloud Gateway: Used exclusively to deliver verification OTP codes and process conversational natural-language expense entries initiated by you.
  • Google Identity Services: Used for optional, user-initiated single-tap sign-in.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You retain absolute ownership and control over your financial records. At any time, you have the right to:

  • Export your complete transaction history and Khata ledgers into portable backup formats.
  • Opt out of automated WhatsApp reminders and notifications via in-app preferences.
  • Request permanent and irreversible deletion of your account and all associated financial records.
