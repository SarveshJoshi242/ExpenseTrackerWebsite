Privacy Policy for Aftermath

Welcome to Aftermath ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Aftermath mobile app, web application, and WhatsApp assistant service.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide and enhance your financial management experience:

  • Account Details: Your name, email address, password hash, and profile avatar when registering an account or signing in via Google.
  • Contact Information: Your mobile phone number for secure WhatsApp OTP verification, SMS transaction syncing, and conversational expense logging.
  • Financial & Expense Records: Transaction amounts, categories (e.g., Food, Travel, Utilities), payment modes (Cash, UPI, Card), Bharat Khata debts, and domestic help wages that you record.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate your monthly budgets, envelope spending limits, and financial analytics.
  • To send you transactional WhatsApp notifications, verification OTP codes, and 9:00 PM daily check-ins via the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API.
  • To authenticate your identity securely using industry-standard JSON Web Tokens (JWT) and Google OAuth.

3. Third-Party Services & Integrations

We partner with trusted third-party providers to power specific application features:

  • Meta WhatsApp Cloud API: Used solely to transmit automated verification OTPs and conversational expense messages initiated by you.
  • Google Identity Services: Used for secure single-tap OAuth authentication.
  • MongoDB Atlas: Encrypted cloud database for persistent storage.

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial data to advertisers or third parties.

4. Data Security

We employ AES-256 encryption at rest, HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, strict password hashing (bcrypt), and rate limiting to safeguard your sensitive information.

5. Data Deletion and Your Rights

You have full ownership of your data. You may delete individual transactions, export your ledger, or request permanent deletion of your account and all associated records at any time by contacting us.
