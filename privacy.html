Privacy Policy

Aftermath is built to give you clear control over your personal finances without sacrificing privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the safeguards protecting your records.

Privacy Pledge: Aftermath never sells, rents, or monetizes your financial transactions or personal details to advertisers or data brokers.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Your name, unique email address, bcrypt-hashed password (never stored in plain text), and optional profile avatar.
  • Mobile Number: Your unique mobile number for WhatsApp OTP verification, password recovery, and optional conversational expense logging.
  • Financial Records: Expenses, income, categories, payment modes, budgets, Bharat Khata entries, and staff records that you add or import.
  • Opt-In Bank Alerts: If you explicitly enable Android Notification Access or share an SMS into Aftermath, only financial transaction alerts are parsed on-device. OTPs, PINs, CVVs, and personal chats are ignored and never stored.

2. Minimal Android Permissions

Aftermath does not request READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS, Camera, Microphone, or external storage permissions. PDF statements are saved directly via Android's scoped MediaStore.Downloads API.

3. Security & Encryption

  • All client-to-server traffic uses HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Database records are isolated per authenticated user account.
  • Each email address and mobile number can only be linked to a single account.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Meta WhatsApp Cloud API: Used solely for verification OTPs, account recovery codes, and user-initiated assistant replies.
  • Google Identity Services: Used for optional single-tap Google Sign-In.

5. Your Rights & Data Export

  • Download full Detailed Financial Reports and filtered Transaction History statements as PDF files at any time.
  • Disable Notification Access or WhatsApp notifications at any time from your device settings.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated financial data via email.
