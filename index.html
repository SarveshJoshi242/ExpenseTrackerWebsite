Designed Without Compromise

Engineered for Indian Financial Realities

Most expense trackers fail because manual data entry is tedious. Aftermath automates the workflow while safeguarding complete privacy.

📩

Native SMS Reader Module

Directly hooks into Android's native Telephony SMS receiver to detect UPI, ATM, and credit card debit alerts. Works 100% locally on your phone without sending SMS data to any cloud.

🤝

Bharat Financial Hub

Complete hisab-kitab ledger for shop udhar, friends lending, and domestic staff salaries (maids, cooks, drivers) with 1-tap WhatsApp payment reminders.

💬

Meta WhatsApp Cloud AI

Powered by the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. No QR scanning needed. Message your spends naturally (e.g. "Chai 20 cash" or "Swiggy 450 upi") and receive automated 9:00 PM check-ins.

⚡

Offline-First Architecture

Zero dependency on internet connectivity. Your ledger resides in high-speed local storage, automatically reconciling with your MongoDB Atlas cloud when online.

🛡️

Multi-Tenant Admin Center

Enterprise dashboard built with React 19 and Vite for overseeing database telemetry, system health, user quotas, and broadcast messaging.

📊

Dynamic Spends & AI Caps

Machine learning algorithms learn your frequent spends over time and surface 1-tap micro pills right on your dashboard for instantaneous logging.
