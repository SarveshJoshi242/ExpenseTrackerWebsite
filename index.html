Security Architecture

Privacy by design, minimal permissions.

Your financial records remain strictly yours — never shared, profiled, or sold.

01

On-Device OTP Firewall

No READ_SMS or RECEIVE_SMS permissions. Opt-in bank alert capture ignores OTPs, MPINs, CVVs, and personal chats before anything is stored.

02

Encrypted Transit & Credentials

All API traffic runs over HTTPS/TLS 1.3. Passwords are salted and hashed with bcrypt, and every user's ledger is isolated by account identity.

03

Single-Account Verification

Strict one-mobile-number and one-email uniqueness prevents duplicate impersonation accounts, backed by 6-digit WhatsApp OTP verification and recovery.
